\section*{Simple Experiment}
Compare DiscoNet with local perception without cooperation and early fusion using compatible checkpoints and identical test scenes.
These baseline strategies are also studied in the OPV2V benchmark~\cite{xu2022opv2v}.
Measure detection quality with Average Precision (AP) at Intersection over Union (IoU) thresholds of 0.5 and 0.7, and show example detections.
The planned desktop has 16 gigabytes (GB) of random-access memory (RAM) and an RTX 5060 Ti graphics processing unit (GPU) with 16 GB of video RAM (VRAM).
Checkpoint availability and software compatibility will be checked first.
